Naming Objects
    Lowercase: functions, variables, scripts, packages
    Uppercase: constants
    Snake Case: functions, variables, scripts, modules (e.g., my_name)

Whitespace
    No whitespace close to parentheses, brackets, or braces
    No whitespace before a comma, semicolon, or colon
    Whitespace after a comma, semicolon, colon, and operators (=, and, ...)
    No whitespace in default values for arguments

Indentations
    Use 4 spaces for one level of indentation
    For long lines, align with the open delimiter, use extra indentation to distinguish, or
    use extra indentation on the conditional continuation line
    Break lines before binary operations (+, -, ...)


Blank Lines
    Functions within the same class: 1 blank line
    Functions, classes, imports, etc.: 2 blank lines
    Separate unrelated functions: 4 blank lines
    Separate logic sections within the same function: 1 blank line
